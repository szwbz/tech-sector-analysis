### 2. Quarterly Run Rate Analysis
**Assumption:** Annual revenue estimate of $350 billion  
**Calculation:** (Q4 2023 Revenue / Annual Revenue Estimate) × 100  
**Result:** Q4 2023 revenue of **$89.5 billion represents 25.57%** of a $350 billion annual run rate

## Interpretation

Apple's Q4 2024 revenue of $94.93 billion represents a solid 6.07% year-over-year growth, demonstrating continued expansion despite macroeconomic challenges. The quarterly revenue represents approximately one-quarter of a projected $350 billion annual run rate, indicating consistent performance across fiscal quarters. This growth was primarily driven by strong iPhone sales and record Services revenue, highlighting Apple's successful diversification beyond hardware into high-margin recurring revenue streams.
